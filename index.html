About Me

I'm Eugene Myburgh, based in Cape Town, South Africa. By day I work as a DEX Technical Lead, but my real passion lives at the intersection of offence and defence in cybersecurity.

This blog is where I break down real-world threats, analyse vulnerabilities, share threat hunting techniques, and explore the ever-changing landscape of information security. Whether it's a new zero-day, a ransomware campaign, or a clever phishing kit — I dig into the details so you don't have to.
